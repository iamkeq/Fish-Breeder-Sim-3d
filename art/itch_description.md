**Fish Breeding Store Sim 3D**

Run your own little fish shop, now in 3D. Walk the shop floor, look into your tanks, and breed better guppies day by day.

**Run the shop**
- Stock the display tank and shelves, then serve customers at the counter. Shoppers browse, admire your fish, and pay at the till.
- Take special orders and back-orders, and win over regulars who come back and tip.
- Close for the day from 13:00, or close for restock to fast-forward breeding.
- Speed the clock up to 2x or 4x when you want the day to move faster.

**Breed**
- Pair guppies in the back room to raise new varieties: red, blue, koi, dalmatian, tuxedo, galaxy and more.
- Choose each tank's food: Growth Flakes raise fry faster, and Spawning Pellets give bigger broods.
- Work towards rare strains in the Lab, record your finds in the breeding book, and enter fish shows.

**Make it yours**
- Decorate tanks with plants, rocks, driftwood, a treasure chest and a stone arch.
- Grow the shop with a second display tank, window boxes, a striped awning, a neon fish sign and a reading corner.
- Hire staff to feed fish and tidy shelves overnight.

**Controls**
- WASD / arrow keys to walk, mouse to look, click or E to use things
- Space to jump, hold Ctrl (or C) to crouch and look into a tank
- T changes the speed, P pauses, F11 toggles full screen, Esc frees the mouse

Built with Godot 4, using art from Synty Studios' POLYGON packs. This is a 3D take on *Fish Breeder Job Simulator*.
